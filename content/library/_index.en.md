The library is one of the significant highlights of St. Mary’s Tamil Academy. It has been established with the aim of fostering interest and a deeper appreciation for the Tamil language, literature, and culture among its members. The library houses a wide variety of Tamil books, including short stories, novels, poetry collections, essays, literary works, grammar books, and other publications.

With several thousand books in its collection, the library serves as a valuable resource for cultivating reading habits and enhancing the members’ linguistic knowledge, literary competence, and creative abilities. In particular, it provides an excellent source of knowledge for future priests, who are called to serve in the Church’s ministry and pastoral mission, enabling them to develop proficiency in the Tamil language and gain a deeper understanding of the richness of Tamil literature and the depth of its cultural heritage. Thus, the Academy Library stands as an important center for intellectual and cultural enrichment, nurturing knowledge through reading, sensitivity through literature, and cultural identity through language.

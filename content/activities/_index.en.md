## Legendary Works of the Academy

The Tamil Academy, with its humble beginning in Kandy, has taken root in the soil of Maharashtra and spread its branches. The Academy’s activities, carried out with the vision of its founders and in keeping with its aims, are many.

1. **Academy Mass:** On the second Friday of every month, the Academy deepens the spiritual life of the seminarians through the Holy Mass.
2. **“Nila” Literary Magazine:** *Nila* continues to shine as a treasury of the seminarians’ thoughts and an outlet for their creative writing. It stands as a record of the Academy’s service and of its members’ growth. *(See the [Nila Magazine Archive](/en/library/nila-magazine/).)*
3. **Tamil Art and Culture:** The Academy makes a distinctive contribution to passing Tamil art and culture to successive generations. Until the 1990s, a Pongal festival was held every year, and cultural programmes and a drama were staged once every two years for the public. Over time, the cultural programmes and dramas were changed to once every three years. They remain an excellent platform for nurturing the artistic talent, creativity, and personality of the brothers. In the academic year 2024–2025, the Academy presented its cultural programme on the theme **“Aram” (Virtue)**.
4. **Support for Mission Work:** In the 1990s, students of theology came together to prepare Stations of the Cross reflections and meditations for the brothers going on mission during the summer and Easter seasons. In the same spirit, the Academy arranges special training in liturgical songs so that the Holy Week services may be conducted with devotion.
5. **Public Speaking and Homiletics:** Seminarians who will be doing their pastoral ministries in Tamil Nadu receive training in public speaking and preaching to prepare for their future Tamil priestly ministry. Literary sessions are also organised every year.
6. **Academy Feast:** The Academy was planted as a small seed and has worked vigorously through many hardships. In thanksgiving to Mother Mary, who has guarded it like the apple of her eye through her ceaseless intercession, the Academy celebrates its feast every year on **8 September**, her birthday.
7. **Pongal Festival:** Every year at the seminary, the Academy celebrates Pongal, the harvest festival of Mother Tamil, on the Tamil festival of *Thai Thirunal*. The celebration includes dance, short cultural programmes, and a traditional banquet featuring around **19 kinds of traditional dishes** prepared for the whole community to celebrate Tamil heritage, culture, and traditional arts.
8. **Ministry in Parishes:** The Academy, which has been crowned in Maharashtra as Madurai was in the south, has not confined itself within the seminary walls. Its beloved children serve in more than ten parishes—including **Ramwadi, Khadki, Shanwadi, Solapur Bazaar, Ghorpuri, Sainikwadi, Dehu Road, Chinchwad, Dharavi, and Wanowrie**. There, our brothers actively preach the Word of God at Sunday liturgies; coordinate choir practice for Sundays, Christmas, Lent, and parish feasts; lead the celebration of *Thai Thirunal* bringing together the Tamil hearts of Pune; and during June (the month of the Sacred Heart) and September (the month of Our Lady of the Rosary), visit families and pray with them, strengthening their spiritual life.

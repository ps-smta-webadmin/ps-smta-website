## Looking Back with Gratitude… and Marching Ahead with Hope
### 125 Years of Service with Smiles (1901–2026)

It is a matter of profound joy that St. Mary’s Tamil Academy of Papal Seminary celebrates its 125th anniversary. Established in Kandy in 1901, the Academy was founded with the aim of helping Tamil-speaking seminarians develop greater proficiency in their mother tongue. After serving the seminarians and the people of God there for several decades, the Academy, along with Papal Seminary, moved to Pune in 1955. Ever since, it has continued to play a significant role in nurturing students’ literary, musical, and dramatic talents, thereby equipping them for more effective pastoral ministry in the future.

To mark this special milestone, a get-together for the Academy’s alumni has been organised on 25 November 2026. We are delighted to welcome our elder brothers back to the Academy, which nurtured them with maternal care, affection, and love. This gathering also coincides with the Centenary Celebrations of Jnana Deepa, commemorating the elevation of Papal Seminary to the status of a Pontifical Athenaeum in 1926, with the right and privilege of conferring the Baccalaureate, Licentiate, and Canonical Doctorate degrees in Philosophy and Theology. This distinction was granted by the Holy See through its charter dated 27 July 1926. Originally established by Pope Leo XIII in Kandy, Sri Lanka, in 1893, Papal Seminary was transferred to Pune, India, in 1955. In 1972, the Pontifical Athenaeum adopted the Indian name Jnana Deepa Institute.

As we look back on the Academy’s remarkable 125-year journey, we bow our heads in reverential gratitude to all our illustrious predecessors who, in one way or another, contributed to its growth and flourishing. Many of them have already received their eternal reward. We, the present generation, must recognise our responsibility to carry forward the rich legacy of this renowned Academy and to make God’s presence felt through our dedicated service to the Church and society.

Let us make the best possible use of the opportunities offered by St. Mary’s Tamil Academy, Papal Seminary, and Jnana Deepa **to grow in love and knowledge**. May our formation equip us to become effective instruments of God’s love and to radiate the Good News to the world. As we celebrate our past with gratitude and look towards the future with hope, let us entrust ourselves and all our endeavours to the powerful intercession of Mary, our Mother, the patroness of the Academy.

In Unity,

**Prof. Stephen (Susainathan) Jayard**<br>
*Animator, St. Mary’s Tamil Academy*<br>
*23 November 2026*

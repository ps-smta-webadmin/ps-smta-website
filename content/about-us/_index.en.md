## The Scope and Aim of the Academy

St. Mary’s Tamil Academy, as an official organization, is meant to help the members to equip themselves suitably for their future ministry as priests, through an all-round formation in Tamil culture and language, and to develop leadership qualities and praxis in pastoral activities. In particular, it is meant to promote proficiency in the pursuit of Tamil language for writing and speaking.

> **Motto of the Academy:** “Anbilum Arivilum Valara” *(To Grow in Love and Wisdom)*
